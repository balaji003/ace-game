Delete your ACE account

ACE: Multiplayer Card Game · ACE — THE LAST HAND

Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  • Open ACE and sign in.
  • Tap the settings gear in the top-right of the lobby.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm with Delete Forever.

Delete by email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, email acegametn@gmail.com from the Google address you signed up with, asking us to delete your ACE account. We action these within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from that address, since it is the only way we can tell the account is yours.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of the following from our database:

  • your account record — username, email address and Google account identifier;
  • your statistics — games played, wins, losses and streaks;
  • your entire game history, including past results and opponents.

This cannot be undone and we keep no backup copy of it afterwards. Signing in again later creates a brand-new, empty account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing that identifies you is retained in the game database after deletion.
  • Server and network access logs — our hosting and security providers record ordinary request metadata, which can include your IP address, as part of standard abuse and security monitoring. These logs are not linked to your account and are discarded automatically within 30 days.
  • Your Google account is unaffected. We never had access to it; deleting your ACE account only removes ACE's own records. To revoke ACE's access to your Google account, visit your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions about deletion, or want a copy of your data first? Email acegametn@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
